# Buy or Wait: engineering overview

Python 3.12; no server or credentials required. Structured ingestion validates joins and dated FX. Deterministic multilingual parsing and local RapidOCR extract provenance-bearing financial facts. Untrusted text cannot choose rules, paths, tools, or decisions. Missing mandatory evidence fails closed rather than becoming zero.

Lifecycle reconciliation anchors current cash at the supplied balance: historical settlements are not replayed, pending credits and unrealized assets are excluded, pending debits are reserved, and supported future income settles on its stated date. Recurrence uses supported histories, effective amendments and stable semantic identities.

Exact Decimal simulation covers request day through day 90 inclusive. The frozen policy uses confirmed same-day credits before payments, preserving opening-floor checks, and a lower median over eight recent protected-variable observations. This is a calibrated estimate, not a guarantee about real-world future spending. Immediate capacity is baseline headroom before optional cuts. Full-payment timing is independent of payment preferences.

The optimizer enumerates accepted full/partial/installment/wait methods and legal flexible changes. Partial payments obey the exact two-payment contract; installments use supplied offers including fees. Candidates are replayed and ranked by deadline, no changes, cost, first payment, count and option ID. A separate decision validator checks eligibility, arithmetic and safety, sharing the exact simulator; it is not an independent economic model.

Production never selects sample expectations or request-specific answers. Public examples are compared only in evaluation scripts. Last public exact scores: safe amount 3/25, status 20/25, method 21/25, plan 20/25, earliest date 20/25, changes 21/25. Safe amounts within 1%: 7/25. These are reused calibration examples, not unseen test performance. Financial safety under the inferred forecast does not prove ground-truth accuracy.

Key limitations: recurrence uncertainty, OCR ambiguity, same-day ordering assumptions, fixed 90-day horizon, and weak exact safe-amount accuracy. No paid model was used in the final offline run. Model-based extraction remains optional for development diagnostics, isolated from authoritative financial arithmetic.
